`System.cause_info`, `effect_info` and `cause_effect_info` with the configured measure are IIT 3.0 quantities. Under any other version of IIT they raise `TypeError` saying so, naming `repertoire_distance=` and pointing to the IIT 4.0 quantities (the intrinsic specification of a mechanism on `System.mic()`, `System.mie()` and `System.intrinsic_specification()`, and of the system on `System.sia()`), instead of failing with an error about dispatching the measure. With an explicit `repertoire_distance` they work under every version, and the measure may be given by its registered name, such as `"KLD"`, as for `System.cause_mip`. Under IIT 3.0 they work as before.
