`System.cause_info`, `effect_info` and `cause_effect_info` are IIT 3.0 quantities. Under any other version of IIT they raise `TypeError` saying so and pointing to the IIT 4.0 quantities (the intrinsic specification of a mechanism on `System.mic()`, `System.mie()` and `System.intrinsic_specification()`, and of the system on `System.sia()`), instead of failing with an error about dispatching the measure. Under IIT 3.0 they work as before.
