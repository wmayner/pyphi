`System.mic`, `mie`, `find_mice` and `distinction` accept the mechanism and each candidate purview as node indices or labels in any order, as `System` does. An unsorted purview such as `(2, 1)` was silently skipped, returning a null result with φ = 0. An unknown label, an index outside the substrate or a unit named twice now raises `ValueError` with a clear message instead of a NumPy `IndexError` or a null result.
