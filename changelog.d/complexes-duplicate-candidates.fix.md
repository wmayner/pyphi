A system named more than once in the `candidates=` argument of `Substrate.complexes`, `maximal_complex`, `all_sias` and `irreducible_sias` (for example as `(1, 2)` and `(2, 1)`, or as a subset and a `System`) is evaluated once, at its first occurrence. Before, the copies tied with each other in the exclusion cascade and excluded each other, so the true complex was dropped and the substrate was reported to have no complex or a weaker overlapping one.
