`AnalyticalRelations` now raises a guided `TypeError` when iterated or indexed — pointing to `.strongest(k)` (top-k relations by φ_r), `.materialize()` (enumerate all), or `config.formalism.iit.relation_computation = "CONCRETE"` (compute the full concrete set) — instead of the bare `"object is not iterable"` / `"not subscriptable"`. The closed-form `len()` still works. (Internally, the two `hasattr(relations, "__iter__")` backend checks in `matching/differentiation.py` and `models/ces.py` now use `isinstance`, since analytical relations gain `__iter__`/`__getitem__`.)
