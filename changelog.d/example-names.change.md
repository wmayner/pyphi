The examples from the 2014 IIT 3.0 paper (Oizumi et al., 2014) carry the prefix `iit3_2014_`, as the examples from later papers carry theirs: `fig1a_substrate()` is now `iit3_2014_fig1a_substrate()`, and likewise for `fig3a`, `fig3b`, `fig4`, `fig5a`, `fig5b`, `fig6`, `fig8`, `fig9`, `fig10`, `fig14` and `fig16`, and for the systems `fig4_system()`, `fig5a_system()` and `fig5b_system()`. Their names in `pyphi.examples.EXAMPLES`, and so in the MCP server's `list_examples` and `load_example`, change the same way (`"fig4"` is now `"iit3_2014_fig4"`). The old names, the PyPhi 1.x names (`fig1a()`, `fig4()`, ..., `actual_causation()`, `prevention()`) and the `*_network`/`*_subsystem` forms raise an `AttributeError` that gives the current name.
