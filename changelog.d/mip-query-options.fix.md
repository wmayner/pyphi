The mechanism-level queries `System.find_mip`, `cause_mip`, `effect_mip`, `phi_cause_mip` and `phi_effect_mip` accept a measure name for `mechanism_measure` and `specification_measure`, as `System.sia` does, raise `MeasureNotCompatibleError` for a measure the active version of IIT does not define, and raise `TypeError` when `state` is not a specified state. `find_mip` and `find_mice` raise `ValueError` for a direction that is not `Direction.CAUSE` or `Direction.EFFECT`, and `System.sia` raises `ValueError` for an unknown `partition_scheme`. The `cause_mip` family now has docstrings.
