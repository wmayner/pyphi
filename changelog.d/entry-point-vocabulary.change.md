The analysis entry points share one argument vocabulary. `analyze` takes `parallel` and `progress` in place of `parallel_kwargs`: `parallel=True` runs one analysis at the system-partition, purview and distinction levels, or a grain search over its candidate systems, and finer tuning goes through the per-level options in `config.infrastructure`. `analyze(grains=False)` means no grain search. `pyphi.macro.complexes`, `intrinsic_units`, `valid_systems`, `is_intrinsic_unit` and `competing_systems` take `parallel` and `progress` in place of `parallel_kwargs`, and `bounds`, `parallel`, `prune` and `progress` are keyword-only. `Transition`'s `partition` and `noise_background` are keyword-only. `optimize(direction=...)` is now `optimize(goal=...)` and `OptimizationResult.direction` is `OptimizationResult.goal`, since `direction` means cause or effect everywhere else in PyPhi; files saved by 2.0.0rc1–rc3 load with the new name. `optimize` builds the substrate at the midpoint of the bounds and the candidate system on it before searching, so a wrong number of bounds, a bad state or an out-of-range `subset` raises its own error instead of scipy's "map-like callable" error. `sweep` accepts the same `compute` values as `analyze` (`None`, `"sia"`, `"ces"`, `"distinctions"` or a callable); its default stays `"sia"`. `estimate_analysis` accepts all of them except a callable, whose cost depends on what the callable does and so cannot be counted in advance. `sweep` no longer takes `seed`, which only labeled provenance; record a seed with `pyphi.provenance.save_dataframe(..., seed=...)`. `phi_posterior` takes `progress`, and `estimate_analysis` and `phi_posterior` take `formalism` like `analyze`.
