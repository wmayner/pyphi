Applying a preset, directly or through `formalism=`, no longer resets `relation_computation`, which chooses how relations are computed but not their values. Setting `pyphi.config.relation_computation = "CONCRETE"` now stays in effect under `formalism=` and under `pyphi.config.override(**pyphi.iit4_2026)`, including when the preset is a copied or unpickled one. An `IITConfig` built with `dataclasses.replace` applies as given even when it equals a preset's, so `pyphi.config.iit = replace(pyphi.config.formalism.iit, relation_computation="ANALYTICAL")` restores closed-form relations, and `pyphi.config["formalism.iit"] = ...` treats a preset the same way as `pyphi.config.iit = ...`.
