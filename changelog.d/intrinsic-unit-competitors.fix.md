The intrinsic-unit criteria now compare a candidate unit only with competitors whose units have an update grain, counted in micro updates, of at most the candidate's own, as Eq. 17 of Marshall et al. (2026) requires. Before, the search judged each decomposition once and applied that verdict at every update grain, so a unit at update grain 1 could be rejected by a competitor built from slower units. With the default `SearchBounds(max_update_grain=1)` every unit has update grain 1 and no result changes. A search with `max_update_grain` above 1 can now find units, and so complexes, that it rejected before. `pyphi.macro.intrinsic_units` reports one verdict per decomposition and update grain, and `DecompositionVerdict` has a new `micro_grain` field holding that grain. `pyphi.macro.competing_systems` and `pyphi.macro.is_intrinsic_unit` apply the same bound using the unit's micro grain.
