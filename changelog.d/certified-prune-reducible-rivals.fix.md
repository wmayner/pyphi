Under `prune="certified"`, the grain search no longer lists candidates whose intrinsic-information ceiling is 0 in `Complex.excluded`. A ceiling of 0 certifies φₛ = 0, so these candidates are reducible and are not rivals of any complex. Gated candidates with a positive ceiling are still listed, with `phi=None`; their φₛ was not computed and may be 0, so the Complex card now counts them in a separate "Gated candidates (φₛ not computed)" row instead of under "Excluded candidates".
