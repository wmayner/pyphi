`pyphi.actual.sia` takes `parallel=` and `progress=` and no other option, and the mechanism-level `System` queries (`find_mip`, `find_mice`, `cause_mip` and the rest) accept `parallel`, `progress`, `chunksize` and `sequential_threshold` as their parallel options. Both used to accept every internal option of the parallel map, including reduce and short-circuit hooks that change the computed result.
