`campaign.prepare` rejects `jobs` below 1 or not a whole number, and `campaign.prepare` and `campaign.prepare_ces` reject a `units_per_job` that is not a positive number. `prepare_ces` rejects an `ordering` other than `"bottleneck_first"` or `None`, which was ignored. Before, `jobs=0` gave one task, `units_per_job=0` raised `ZeroDivisionError` and a misspelled ordering had no effect.
