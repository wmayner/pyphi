The `candidates=` argument of `Substrate.complexes`, `maximal_complex`, `all_sias` and `irreducible_sias` accepts subsets of units (indices or labels) as well as `System` objects, and rejects a `System` of another substrate or state. A flat sequence of indices or labels, such as `candidates=(0, 1)` or `candidates=("A", "B")`, is one candidate, as `subsets=` is in `pyphi.sweep`; release candidates read `("A", "B")` as the two one-unit candidates `A` and `B`. A string is rejected with a `TypeError`, as candidates and as a subset (`analyze(..., subset="AB")`), where it used to be split into one-character labels.
